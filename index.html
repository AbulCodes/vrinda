Dear Vrinda, ❤️

Happy Girlfriend Day, my Queen.

There are thousands of words in this world, yet none of them truly describe how grateful I am that you came into my life. Every day with you, even from miles away, has become a memory I treasure.

People say every queen deserves a king, but I don't think that's our story.

You're my Queen, and I'm your Soldier.

Not because I stand below you, but because loving you means standing beside you with loyalty, protecting your smile, supporting your dreams, and never giving up on us. If life ever becomes difficult, I want to be the one standing with you through every battle.

Thank you for being patient with me, for understanding me, for making ordinary days feel special, and for becoming the person I look forward to talking to every single day.

Even though we're far apart, you've never felt far from my heart. Every sunrise reminds me that somewhere under the same sky, my favorite person is smiling. And that's enough to make my day better.

I don't know what the future holds. I don't know where life will take us.

If I had to choose again, in every lifetime, I'd still choose you.

I promise to respect you.
I promise to trust you.
I promise to support your dreams as if they were my own.
I promise to stay honest with you.

I promise that no amount of distance will ever make me stop loving you.

One day we'll look back at these long-distance days and smile because we made it through together.

Until that day comes, I'll keep counting every day we've been together, every conversation we've had, every laugh we've shared, and every memory we're yet to create.

Thank you for being my happiness, my peace, and the most beautiful part of my life.

Happy Girlfriend Day, my Queen. ❤️

Forever Yours,

Your Soldier,
